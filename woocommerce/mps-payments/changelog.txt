*** MPS Payments for WooCommerce Changelog ***

2026-10-22 - version 0.1.0
* First release: payments on the MPS-hosted checkout, block and classic checkout, Dutch, French and English.
* Payment notifications are acted on only when they carry a valid signature; without a webhook signing secret they are ignored and orders are settled by the plugin's own checks.
* Tested with WordPress 7.0 and 7.1 and WooCommerce 11.2.
